RawCopyUtil

Copy the RAW originals that match your selected JPGs.

A macOS utility for photographers. After your client picks their favorites, RawCopyUtil matches those JPG files with your camera's RAW originals by filename and safely copies only the ones you need.

Support Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-09-04

RawCopyUtil does not collect any personal information.

Data Collection

None. The app has no accounts, no login, no analytics, and no advertising SDKs, and it does not communicate with any server.

File Access

The app only accesses photo files (RAW/JPG) in folders you explicitly select. All processing — filename matching, copying, verification — happens locally on your Mac. No files or file information ever leave your device.

Logs

Diagnostic logs are stored only inside the app's sandbox on your Mac and are deleted automatically after 30 days. They are never transmitted anywhere unless you export them yourself.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We never see or store your payment information.

Questions about this policy? Email us at soyoudd@naver.com
